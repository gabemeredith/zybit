What Quill handles

Everything a self-employed filer needs, no upsells, no surprise paywalls. Here's the list.

Start filing

1099-NEC import

Connect your bank and Quill identifies 1099-NEC deposits automatically. Cross-references against the actual 1099 forms when they arrive in January.

QBI deduction calc

The 20% Qualified Business Income deduction is the single biggest tax break for self-employed filers. Quill computes it correctly across SSTB and non-SSTB activities.

Multi-state filing

Lived in two states this year? Earned 1099 income from a third? Hustle and Empire plans handle it cleanly, including allocation rules per state.

Quarterly estimates worksheet

The IRS wants estimated payments four times a year. Quill generates the worksheet for next year as part of this year's filing, with the actual amounts and due dates pre-filled.
