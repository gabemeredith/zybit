Northwind in 90 seconds

Connect your warehouse with a read-only role. Northwind starts surfacing per-query attribution within the first hour. Slack alerts arrive after we have a baseline (24 hours of data).

1. Create the read-only role

In your warehouse, run the included role-creation script (we provide it for Snowflake, BigQuery, and Redshift). The script grants Northwind read access to query history and account usage views. No write access anywhere.

2. Connect Slack

One-click Slack OAuth. Northwind posts alerts to a channel of your choice — default is #data-cost-alerts. You can route by team or by warehouse later.

3. Set budget thresholds

Optional but recommended. Tell Northwind your monthly budget by team or by warehouse. We page proactively at 50%, 75%, and 90% — not after the fact.
