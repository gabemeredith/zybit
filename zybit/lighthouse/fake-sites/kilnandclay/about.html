The Studio

Kiln & Clay is a two-person ceramics studio in SE Portland. Mara handles the wheel — fifteen years on a Brent CXC, mostly stoneware, mostly functional. Joon handles glaze chemistry — ceramics MFA, materials background, the one who fixes Mara's recipes when they go wrong.

What we make

Functional stoneware: mugs, bowls, vessels, the occasional serving piece. We work in seasonal drops of forty to sixty pieces, four or five times a year, plus a small standing line of mugs and bowls we restock continuously.

How we make it

Clay is wedged by hand, thrown on the wheel, trimmed once leather-hard, bisque-fired to cone 06, glazed by hand-dip or brush, and reduction-fired to cone 10 in our gas kiln. Two firings, four to seven days per piece from wedge to ship.

Visit the studio

The studio is open for pickups and small studio visits on Thursdays and Saturdays. Drop us a note before you come — it's a small space and we throw most days.
