Everyday banking, finally simple

AcmeBank Checking gives you a fee-free debit card, mobile check deposits, and an account you can open in under three minutes — all backed by FDIC insurance up to $250,000.

Over 1.2 million people use AcmeBank to handle their everyday spending, get paid up to two days early, and avoid the overdraft surprises that traditional banks build their business on.

What's included

A Mastercard debit card shipped to you the same day you open your account. Direct deposit with no minimum balance. Mobile check deposit straight from the app. Bill pay and instant transfers to friends and family who also bank with us.

You'll get access to more than 55,000 fee-free ATMs across the country through our partnership with Allpoint, plus free incoming wires and no foreign transaction fees on international purchases.

No monthly fees. No minimums. No surprises.

We don't charge a monthly maintenance fee, a minimum-balance fee, or a fee to talk to a real human. There's no overdraft fee either — if you don't have the money, the transaction just doesn't go through, the way it should.

We make money on interchange (the small fee merchants pay when you swipe your card) and on the difference between what we earn on your deposits and what we pay you in interest. That's it.

Get paid up to two days early

When your employer sends your direct deposit, we make it available as soon as we get the notice from the ACH network — typically one to two business days before payday. No opt-in, no extra account, no catch.

More than 60% of our members tell us early direct deposit is the single biggest reason they switched to AcmeBank.

FDIC insured up to $250,000

AcmeBank, N.A. is a Member FDIC, so deposits in your checking account are insured up to the standard $250,000 limit per depositor, per ownership category. Your money is safe even on the days when the internet is on fire.

We use bank-level 256-bit AES encryption in transit and at rest, and every account requires multi-factor authentication on every login from a new device.

Built for the way you actually spend

Real-time spending notifications hit your phone before the merchant's terminal has finished printing the receipt. You can freeze your card from the app the second it goes missing and unfreeze it just as fast if it turns up in your jacket pocket.

Categorized spending insights, recurring-charge tracking, and a budget that updates automatically as transactions clear — all included, no premium tier.
