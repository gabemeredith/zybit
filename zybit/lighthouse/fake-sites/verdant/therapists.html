Our therapists

Every Verdant therapist is licensed in the state they practice and credentialed by our clinical team — minimum five years of post-license experience, ongoing supervision, and a clear specialty area.

Specialties we cover

Anxiety, depression, ADHD, grief, life transitions, relationships, burnout, perinatal, LGBTQ-affirming care, trauma-focused CBT, EMDR, ACT, IFS, and DBT-informed work.

Modalities

Video, phone, secure messaging. Most clients pick video plus messaging between sessions; some prefer phone-only. You can switch modalities at any time.

Diverse perspectives

Our network includes therapists of color, LGBTQ+ therapists, and clinicians who specialize in cultural-identity work. You can request a specific demographic match on the intake.
