How matching works

Tell us about what you're looking for and a clinical-care coordinator (not an algorithm) reads your intake and pairs you with two or three therapists you can choose from. Most clients start their first session within four days.

Insurance, billed honestly

We're in-network with Aetna, Cigna, BlueCross, and most state Medicaid plans. We'll show you your estimated copay before you book — no surprise bills, no "we'll figure it out later."

Who Verdant is for

People dealing with anxiety, depression, grief, life transitions, relationship issues, ADHD, and burnout. We're not a fit for active psychosis, eating disorders requiring inpatient care, or current substance-use rehab — we'll refer out honestly if so.
