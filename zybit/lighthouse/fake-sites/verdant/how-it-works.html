How Verdant works

From the moment you fill out the intake to your first session is typically four to seven days. Here's the full sequence.

1. Tell us what you're looking for

A short intake form: what you're working through, what kind of therapist you'd like to be matched with, your insurance, and when you can meet. About four minutes.

2. A coordinator reads it

Not an algorithm — a licensed clinical coordinator pairs you with two or three therapists in our network whose specialties, modalities, and availability match what you wrote.

3. Pick one and book

We send the matches within 24 hours with bios and availability. You pick the one that feels right and book your first session straight from the email.
