Your Verdant intake

A short form so our clinical coordinator can match you well. About four minutes. Your answers are encrypted end-to-end and only seen by clinical staff.

Our clinical coordinator reviews your intake personally — not an algorithm — and emails you two or three matched therapists within 24 hours. You pick one and book directly.
